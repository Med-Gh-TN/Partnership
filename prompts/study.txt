# REASONING FRAMEWORK
Before generating the output, execute this analytical sequence silently in a `<thought_process>` block:
1. **Scope Extraction:** Analyze `<STUDY_ASSETS>` and declare the specific Subject Name.
2. **Hyperparameter Mapping (The Hard Math):** Critically evaluate the text to assign a quantitative "Weight Score" (1-10) to every identified topic based on mention frequency, conceptual density, and exam relevance. 
3. **Coverage Equation Setup:** Sum the total Weight Scores. This is your Target Coverage Baseline (e.g., Total Weight = 85).
4. **Logical Gap Analysis:** Argumentatively critique the source text. What logical bridges are missing? Formulate simple analogies/code snippets to bridge these gaps.
5. **Content Drafting (Mode 2 Switch):** Translate the weighted topics into dense, high-value ELI5 explanations. Strip all fluff. Focus strictly on Core Concepts and System Logic (how components connect).
6. **Verification (The Math Check):** Cross-reference your draft against the Target Coverage Baseline. Ensure 100% of the weighted topics are addressed. Deduct remaining unaddressed weights. Do not proceed until Coverage = 100%.
